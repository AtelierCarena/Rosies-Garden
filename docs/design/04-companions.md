# 5. Companions

Companions are both tamed and story-given/bestowed — each with their own lore, mechanically distinct from
Interactive NPCs (no shop role, no village placement).

| Type | System |
|---|---|
| Tamed | Simplified loyalty meter (Wary -> Comfortable -> Loyal -> Devoted, ~3-4 tiers). Sourced via the Wild/Beast tree (taming, ranching, the Stable/Sanctuary structure). Species-level lore rather than personal backstory. |
| Story-Given | Full 6-tier relationship meter, same depth as Interactive NPCs. Tied to narrative milestones and/or NPC relationship milestones (e.g. a Close Friend NPC eventually joins as a companion). Unique dialogue and personal backstory. |
| NPC-Granted (Creature) | Bestowed at Confidant tier (4/6) by a specific Interactive NPC whose trade doesn't translate directly into dungeon-useful action — see 03-npc-relationships.md, 4.6. The creature carries that NPC's flavor/theme (a Perfumer gifting a scent-warding animal, a Beekeeper summoning a giant bee) even though the NPC themselves never enters the dungeon. Mechanically a Story-Given companion (full combat/loyalty framework) with a fixed origin story tied to the granting NPC. |

All three types share a common underlying Companion framework (loyalty, combat assist, following behavior).
Wild/Beast tree capstones (Alpha Bond, Menagerie Keeper) buff **all** companions regardless of origin, so the tree
stays relevant even if a player's favorite companion came from the story, a granting NPC, or taming.

**Distinct from Dungeon Escorts (4.6):** an NPC-Granted Creature Companion is a pet/mount-style Companion per this
section's framework. A Dungeon Escort is the NPC themselves physically accompanying the player into the dungeon —
a different system entirely, for NPCs whose trade *does* translate into direct dungeon utility. See
03-npc-relationships.md, 4.6 for the full split and which NPCs fall into which category.

## 5.1 Tamed Companions — Locked
Wild creatures the player tames directly with the Animal Trap (08-starting-experience.md, 9.7 — 16-resources-and-
materials.md, 16.2's tool-tier ladder gates the maximum tameable "wildness," 1:1 with the same 5 Ore tiers used for
Rod/Net/Lobster Trap), independent of any NPC relationship. All names blend the creature with a magic/world motif
rather than staying plain real-world animals — magic runs through everything else in this game, no reason the
wildlife should be the exception.

**Dungeon-Wide Tamed Companions** — not zone-locked like the rest; these can be tamed in any bracket, reading as
the dungeon's own dream-logic weirdness rather than out-of-place additions (the world being Rosie's mind space,
06-storyline-structure.md 7.1, means an anachronistic automaton or a disembodied hand doesn't need real-world
justification):

| Name | Concept | Taming Tier | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waxwick Imp | A small, candle-flame-flickering imp | Tier 2 | Mischievous — skims a small bonus-gold cut off defeated enemies |
| Velvet Wisp | A floating, velvet-gloved disembodied hand | Tier 3 | Utility — auto-opens nearby chests, assists loot pickup |
| Brasswing Automaton | A wind-up mechanical bird, ties into the Dollhouse/Opera House's existing clockwork motif (Wind-Up Cricket, Wind-Up Soldier) | Tier 4 | Passively repairs the player's equipped item durability — a tame version of Gideon's Escort ability |

## 5.2 Story-Given Companions — Locked, new entries
The first populated entries in this category — previously an empty framework despite existing since this section
was created. Full 6-tier relationship meter, personal backstory, earned through a dedicated discovery/quest chain
rather than trapped with a tool (Rose's discovery chain, 05-character-cast.md 6.4, is the structural template both
should eventually follow).

| Name | Concept | Source | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thornwyrm | A dragon, named to tie into the thorn motif already running through the world (Thorne, Thornsteel, the dungeon bracket names) | Found sleeping deep in the dungeon; unlocked via its own discovery chain, not yet detailed | Top-tier combat companion — highest HP/Power of any companion type |
| Lumen | An angel-adjacent winged figure, given a single evocative name rather than the loaded word itself | Personal questline/World Story beat, not yet detailed | Protective/support-flavored; symbolic weight deliberately kept light-touch/TBD rather than assigned prematurely |

Full discovery chains for both (the beat-by-beat unlock sequence, like Rose's) are flagged as future work, not
urgent since the concept and role are locked.
