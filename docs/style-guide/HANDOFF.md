## Character roster (as briefed so far)

Character personalities/roles/species are **not yet fully briefed** — only visual placeholders exist so far. Fill in job/personality/lore as it's decided; the body-type assignment in `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §4 depends on this.

### Nix
Fae/fairy type character. Butterfly-style wings (lilac/purple, veined). Red hair in space-buns + long pale blonde/white hair on one side (two-tone split). Sage-green head wrap with a small bow. Freckles, dark red/pink eyes. Dark maroon dress, gothic-style choker and pendant necklace. Moody dark-forest backdrop in the reference — reads as the most literal "gloomy coquette" character of the set.
*Placeholder source: third-party avatar generator, not a final design.*

### Olivier
Elf (long pointed ears). Messy dark auburn/brown wavy hair, center part. Round wire-frame glasses. Amber/gold eyes. Light stubble, small scar/mark near one eye, freckles. Black button-up shirt with tie. Reads scholarly/reserved — a good contrast to Nix's fae whimsy.
*Placeholder source: third-party avatar generator, not a final design.*

### Silas
**Needs redesign** — current placeholder has cat-like ears/nose because the generator tool used had no otter option. Actual character should be otter-featured (rounder ears, whiskers, otter-shaped nose/snout, possibly a stockier/more athletic build per the body-type-per-species rule in the style guide). Otherwise: warm brown wavy hair, blue eyes, rectangular glasses, tan skin, black turtleneck.
*Placeholder source: third-party avatar generator — ears/nose are wrong and must be corrected in the final design.*

### Bella
Human. Voluminous curly brown hair with two-tone bleached/blonde box braids framing the face. Small dark/white horn-shaped hair clips or bows. Rectangular glasses. Gray-blue eyes, tan skin. Layered necklaces with a pendant charm. Oversized graphic tee/off-shoulder top. Alt/grunge aesthetic — a deliberate contrast to the more "fantasy-fancy" cast members.
*Placeholder source: third-party avatar generator, not a final design.*

### Leonair
Elf (long pointed ears), mage-coded. Tan skin, pale blonde hair, violet/purple sparkly eyes, rectangular glasses. Scruffy beard with a small braided chin-tuft. Wide dark navy witch/wizard hat, green-and-navy jacket. **Needs more "magical" styling** than the placeholder tool could provide — more ornate hat detailing, glowing/arcane accents, richer robe/costume treatment.
*Placeholder source: third-party avatar generator, not a final design.*
